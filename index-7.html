Written by Panchangam Sameera

UNTITLED

I finally decided to say it… and now I can’t.

Tap to begin
BLACK SCREEN — TITLE — CHARACTER NAMES

In the background, Rishi speaks to Rahul over a phone call.

RISHI (V.O.)

Arey I got it raa. I have edited the final report. I will meet you near the xerox shop in 10 minutes bye.

INT. RISHI ROOM

Rishi stands at his desk, packing his bag with his laptop, charger and headphones. He closes the zipper. Amma calls from outside.

AMMA (O.S.)

Eey Rishi, Tiffin ready aythu baaro

RISHI

bande amma

He rushes out — then suddenly remembers something. He returns to the desk, opens the drawer, takes out a neatly folded letter, puts it in the bag and walks outside.

✉ The letter enters the day
INT. DINING HALL

Rishi drinks milk and keeps the glass on the table. Amma calls again.

AMMA (O.S.)

Eey, sink alli hako bandhu, adike obru beka ninge

With irritation, he picks the glass and walks into the kitchen where Amma is cooking. He puts the glass in the sink.

RISHI

Amma time agidhe, innu xerox tegisbeku.

AMMA

Eey Rishi, xerox ge duddu bedeno,

He realises, turns back to her with a smile.

RISHI

hushh… correct alva

AMMA

Appa table mele itidare nodu

RISHI

Thank you sweet lady

He teases her, grabs the money, then the keys.

Camera on the keys as he grabs them.
CUT TO:
EXT. XEROX SHOP
Camera on Rishi's hands and the keys on the handle.
RAHUL

Finally, bro… how much time you take bro

As Rishi parks, Rahul walks over, grabs his bag and heads back to the xerox.

RISHI

Eey Rahul, I have directly shared the PDF to Shiva Anna, check madu alli.

RAHUL

Arey kukha… should have told right

Rishi laughing shot.

Behind him, Rahul gets the xerox done and talks about the binding while Rishi dials Ranjith.

RAHUL
slight Kannada accent due to language shift

Shiva Anna, CS colour, binding, cream colour haki

Ranjith's caller tune is heard low: “Eey mast mast hudugi bandlu.” The call connects and the screen splits.

LEFT — Rishi on phone, Rahul in background. RIGHT — Ranjith sleeping on bed, phone beside him on speaker.
RANJITH
sleepy

Hey, ello macha, any problem!!?

RISHI

Lae thika muckond come fast macha, HOD sign marthoda…

Ranjith gets up violently.

RANJITH

Wvoo correct alva hitler mama sign… sorry bro I will come asap

The split screen returns to single, switching to Ranjith.

INT. RANJITH ROOM

He checks the phone: 3 missed calls from Rahul, 25 minutes ago. 1 missed call from Veda, 15 minutes ago.

He texts Veda:

RANJITH

Sorry, just got up. I am coming

Small montage — bath, clothes, bag, out the door.

RANJITH

Bye maa

RANJITH'S AMMA (O.S.)

eey tiffin kano, yavaglu late ready agodhu hange hogadu

RANJITH

Bandmele tintini maa

EXT. RANJITH HOUSE

He walks out, gets on the bike and starts it.

With the engine revving sound, the scene cuts.
EXT. XEROX

Ranjith arrives, parks, takes his phone out and walks to the other two.

RISHI

Finally bossu bandru…

RISHI

Dude this is the last date for signing, and your hitler mama, aagle namma mele tumba vale opinion ide

RANJITH

Relax bro, Aagle Veda is in college she texted me

Hearing her name, Rishi's face brightens up — then returns to normal.

RAHUL

It is 765 kanro per person.

Rahul pats Rishi on the back.

RAHUL

Ammai peru vinagane eh

RANJITH

Lae, again with this shit. Artha aaguva bashe matador…

RAHUL

Abbo, sare bidu, give 765 now

Rishi laughs and hands the cash to Rahul. The bindings go into the bag.

RAHUL

Shiva Anna togoli, uldidu phonepe agide

SHIVA ANNA

Ok kanrappa

EXT. COLLEGE PARKING

Rishi gives the bike keys to Rahul. The three walk to the bikes. Ranjith starts his bike and immediately moves across the road while they follow.

RAHUL

En maga, urgent avndhu.

RISHI

Hitler mama bhaya irbeku

They enter college parking. Ranjith is already talking to someone on the parking steps. As Rishi gets closer, he sees who it is.

It is Veda.
And suddenly, something shifts in him.

Rishi and Rahul park, get down and walk toward them.

RAHUL

nee pilla mawa

Rishi turns to him with frustration and hidden anxiety.

RISHI
low voice

She will get to know raa

RAHUL

That’s why I switched the language. It is our advantage raa vaduko

Rishi tells him to shut up with a hand action and meets them.

VEDA

Hi Rishi, got the xerox?

RISHI

Yeah Veda, tadi,

He unzips the bag, takes out all four copies and hands them to Veda.

RISHI

check madu once. Again hod sir will scold

RANJITH

Abba, siru…. Aagle you called him hitler mama

RISHI

Lae dude, we are in college methige…

VEDA

Ranjith, stop it. Lets get them signed first. First class ge hogi we will check once, and then sign madsake hogana

RANJITH

Ok teacher, nivu henge andre hange

All four laugh and walk from the parking to the classroom.

INT. CLASSROOM

Veda and Ranjith sit on one bench. Rishi and Rahul sit on another, facing them. Veda goes through the report. Rahul scrolls on his phone. Ranjith is on his phone.

Rishi takes his bag and grabs the letter. He keeps it in one hand, hidden off the desk.

✉ Still here
VEDA

Everything looks correct kanro.

Rishi looks at her as she starts to speak.

A notification sounds from her phone. Veda checks it and keeps it down.

VEDA

Let’s do one thing, hey Rahul, go and check na if HOD is available in the office

RAHUL

Abba…,,

Rahul gets up. He looks at Rishi. Rishi signs him to take Ranjith.

RISHI
low voice, with sign

vadni, vadni eskapo

Rahul pats Rishi's shoulder, gives him a head shake and calls Ranjith.

RAHUL

Lae Ranji trophy, come macha, nodkond barana

They both get up and walk toward the door. Veda follows their movement. Rishi watches her, then them, as they leave.

VEDA

Hey Rishi, I have to ask you something

Rishi cannot contain the excitement and anxiety at once. He straightens himself, trying to behave normally.

RISHI

Yeah, wahtsup??

One hand stays off the desk holding the letter. The other plays with the report pages.

VEDA

Adu, I want to tell you guys I mean you an…

Rishi's phone rings — Salaar FRIENDS BGM ringtone.
RISHI

wvo Ranjith call madthidane, wait a second dude

RISHI

Ello macha, HOD available?

RISHI

Ohh howda… wait a second bande

He ends the call, gets up and puts the phone and the letter in his pocket.

RISHI

Yoo, wait a second Veda, Ranjith is calling. I will go and check out.

VEDA

Yeah, ok sure, it can wait.

As Rishi walks out, he hears the notification sound again. He looks at Veda. She smiles and keeps the phone down. He says nothing and walks through the hallway to the washroom.

INT. WASHROOM

Rahul and Ranjith are talking. Ranjith is happy. Rahul looks at Rishi with a sorry look while still holding the happy face. Rishi looks at him and silently asks what.

RANJITH

Yoo dude, you came. First tell me you also won’t laugh

RISHI

Yoo, mathe en madidro, HOD room lock haki bandra hege, hahaha…

RANJITH

Dude, I am serious here…

Rishi leans against the wall near the wash basin.

Rahul is caught between emotions — smiling, but looking at Rishi with sympathy.

Ranjith walks to Rishi and keeps a hand on his shoulder. Happiness in his eyes. Affection.

Macha I am in love with Veda.
And she also has the same feeling.

Rishi goes blank. Still. Disconnected from the world. Ranjith's voice turns to echoes.

RANJITH

Yoo, bro… u there?? Lae Rahul eno heart attack enadru aytha ivnige

RAHUL

Arey Rishi, red flower…

Rahul shakes him violently. Rishi comes back. He looks at Ranjith and suddenly starts laughing out loud, unable to control it. He moves to the corner, turns back, pauses — his eyes filled with tears, happy and sad at the same time — then laughs again.

RANJITH

Lae, what a respectable friends kanro…

Rishi pats his shoulder.

RISHI

What did you think, helid takshna film alli tara bandu hug madbek itha… silly fellow.

RISHI

Rey Rahul kumar unada kanuko raa

RANJITH

Bros translate, kumar yaake iga

RISHI

Abba, ist good news heli party beku iga…. Kumar gobi

RISHI

nuvvu call kotu mawa

Ranjith's phone rings.

RANJITH

Ha yeah Veda, ..

RANJITH

illa ila bandvi, we are done

RANJITH

Ah okk, ok

RANJITH

Hey come guys lets go, Hitler is free anthe iga, lets get this done..

RISHI

Hey you guys go, I will pee and come

RANJITH

Yeah ok, lae baro hulla

Ranjith leaves. Rishi looks at Rahul through the mirror — with the real face. Rahul pats him on the shoulder and stands beside him.

RANJITH (O.S.)

Lae Rahul baaro

RAHUL

bande tadiyo….

Rahul pats Rishi's shoulder again and walks out.

INT. WASHROOM — CONTINUOUS
Only Rishi remains.

He stares at the mirror. His eyes fill again. He violently washes his face. One water droplet moves from his eyes, but he holds a smile.

He goes to the dustbin, takes the letter out, holds it tightly…

…before tearing it.

He puts it in the dustbin.

✉ The letter leaves the day
MONTAGE BEGINS

Rishi takes out his earbuds, plugs them in, turns Bluetooth on and starts to play the song.

Important shot composition: Rishi stays in the foreground, near camera, around shoulder level. The earbuds remain visible. The music belongs to him; he is the only character hearing it.
MONTAGE — FIRST STANZA

Friends eat Gobi, laugh and enjoy. Ranjith and Veda are closer than usual now — comfortable enough to be open.

Rishi walks away to put the used plates in the trash. A hint of tears. He wipes them swiftly, smiles, turns back to his friends.

They go to the bus stop. Rahul catches the bus. Ranjith and Veda leave on his bike. Rishi waves both of them goodbye.

Then he goes to start his own bike.

MONTAGE — RIDE HOME

Rishi rides back home. With nobody left to perform for, every emotion flows out of his eyes. No filter now. Nothing held inside.

Camera mounted on the front of the bike, capturing his face all the way home.
INT. RISHI ROOM — LATER

Rishi opens the room door.

He puts the bag on the chair.

Walks to the bed.

Falls onto it.

And goes to sleep.
